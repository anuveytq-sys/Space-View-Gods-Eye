# Data Sources, Licenses & Attribution

God's Eye View fuses diverse real-time geospatial intelligence, satellite telemetry, aviation/maritime tracking, environmental sensors, and 3D terrain feeds.

See the full original document for per-source license and attribution details. Key sources include:

- Google Photorealistic 3D Tiles / Cesium ion
- OpenStreetMap / Carto
- OpenSky Network / adsb.lol (ADS-B)
- AISStream (maritime)
- CelesTrak (satellites)
- NASA FIRMS (wildfires)
- USGS (earthquakes)
- Various municipal CCTV and open data feeds

Third-party data remains under their respective licenses. See original DATA_SOURCES.md in the source tree for complete tables.
